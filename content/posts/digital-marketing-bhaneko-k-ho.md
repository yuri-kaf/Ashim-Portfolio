"डिजिटल मार्केटिङ भनेको के हो?" पहिलो मिटिङमै धेरै business owner ले यो प्रश्न सोध्नुहुन्छ, कहिलेकाहीँ अलिक लाज मानेर। लाज मान्नुपर्ने कुरै छैन। धेरैले यसलाई "Facebook मा post boost गर्नु" भनेर मात्र बुझेका छन्। त्यसैले पैसा त खर्च हुन्छ, तर ग्राहक त्यति बढ्दैनन्।

यो लेखमा म सजिलो भाषामा बताउँछु: डिजिटल मार्केटिङ के हो, नेपालमा कुन माध्यम चल्छन्, पहिलो ९० दिनमा के गर्ने, र काम भयो कि भएन कसरी थाहा पाउने। हरेक भागमा पहिले नेपाली, त्यसपछि English छ।

A lot of business owners in Kathmandu ask me some version of this in the first meeting, usually a little apologetically: digital marketing bhaneko k ho, exactly? It's a fair question. Most people have only met it as "boost the post on Facebook", which is how a lot of money goes out without many more customers coming in.

Here's the plain answer, each section in Nepali first and then English: what it is, which channels matter in Nepal, a first 90-day plan, and how to tell whether it's working.

## डिजिटल मार्केटिङ भनेको के हो? / What digital marketing is

सजिलो भाषामा भन्दा, इन्टरनेट प्रयोग गरेर सही मान्छेलाई तपाईंको business बारे थाहा दिनु, भरोसा दिलाउनु, अनि उनीहरूलाई फोन, message वा अर्डर गर्न लगाउनु नै डिजिटल मार्केटिङ हो।

Google मा तपाईंको पसल खोज्दा आउने जानकारी, तपाईंको website, Facebook र Instagram page, TikTok को video, Viber मा पुराना ग्राहकलाई पठाएको offer, सबै यसैभित्र पर्छन्। Boost गर्नु यसको एउटा सानो भाग मात्र हो।

पत्रिकाको विज्ञापनभन्दा फरक के छ भने, यहाँ धेरै कुरा नाप्न सकिन्छ: कतिले हेरे, कतिले message गरे, र हिसाब राख्नुभयो भने कतिले किने।

Plainly put, digital marketing is using the internet to get the right people to hear about your business, trust it, and then call, message or order.

Your details on Google when someone searches for your shop, your website, your Facebook and Instagram pages, a TikTok video, an offer you send regulars on Viber: all of it counts. Boosting a post is one small tool inside it, not the whole job.

The real difference from a newspaper ad or a flex board is that most of it can be measured: how many people saw it, how many messaged, and, if you keep track, how many bought.

## नेपालमा ग्राहक अनलाइन कहाँ छन्? / Where Nepali customers are online

DataReportal को [Digital 2026: Nepal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-nepal) रिपोर्टअनुसार (२०२५ को अन्त्यसम्मको तथ्यांक) नेपालमा करिब १ करोड ६६ लाख इन्टरनेट प्रयोगकर्ता छन्, अर्थात् जनसंख्याको ५६ प्रतिशत। Social media मा १ करोड ४८ लाख user identity छन्, तर एउटै मान्छेका धेरै account पनि हुन सक्छन्। मोबाइल connection चाहिँ ३ करोड २४ लाख छन्, जनसंख्याभन्दा पनि बढी, किनकि धेरैसँग एकभन्दा बढी SIM हुन्छ। आधा जनसंख्या करिब २५ वर्षभन्दा कम उमेरको छ, र ७७ प्रतिशत मान्छे ग्रामीण क्षेत्रमा बस्छन्।

Platform हरूकै ad tool अनुसार Facebook मा १ करोड ४८ लाख, Messenger मा १ करोड १० लाख, Instagram मा ४३ लाख ५० हजार र LinkedIn मा २३ लाख मान्छेसम्म विज्ञापन पुग्न सक्छ।

मैले देखेअनुसार धेरैजसो सानो business का लागि Facebook र Messenger नै मुख्य पसल हो। Instagram लुगा, खाना, cafe र beauty जस्ता देखाएर बेचिने सामानका लागि राम्रो छ। TikTok र YouTube बाट नयाँ मान्छेले तपाईंलाई भेट्छन्। Google मा चाहिँ के किन्ने भनेर पक्का भइसकेका मान्छे खोज्दै आउँछन्। अनि Viber, SMS र email ले एक पटक किनिसकेका ग्राहकलाई फेरि बोलाउँछन्। लगभग सबै कुरा मोबाइलमै हुन्छ, र सहरबाहिर internet अझै ढिलो छ। त्यसैले तपाईंको website र video कमजोर signal मा पनि खुल्नुपर्छ।

DataReportal's [Digital 2026: Nepal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-nepal) report, using data to the end of 2025, puts Nepal at about 16.6 million internet users, 56% of a population of 29.6 million. There are 14.8 million social media user identities (not the same as unique people, since one person can have several accounts) and 32.4 million mobile connections, 109% of the population, because plenty of people carry more than one SIM.

The ad reach the platforms themselves report gives a sense of scale: 14.8 million on Facebook, 11.0 million on Messenger, 4.35 million on Instagram and 2.30 million on LinkedIn. That's what the ad tools say they can reach, not a headcount, but the order is useful. The same report puts the median age at 25.3, with 77% of people living in rural areas. Your customers are young, and a lot of them are not in Kathmandu.

How the channels tend to work in practice:

- **Facebook and Messenger** are still the shopfront for most small businesses. People find you, check your page and message to ask the price.
- **Instagram** suits anything that sells on looks, like clothing, food, cafés and beauty.
- **TikTok and YouTube** are where new people discover you.
- **Google Search and Maps** catch people who already know what they want.
- **Your website** holds the full, trustworthy version: what you offer, prices, location, contact.
- **Viber, SMS and email** bring back past customers.

Nearly all of this happens on a phone. Nepal Telecommunications Authority figures [reported by the Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/science-technology/2026/04/14/4g-keeps-growing-fast-in-nepal-while-users-still-face-slow-speeds-and-patchy-coverage) show 4G accounts for 86.24% of broadband subscriptions, but users still deal with slow speeds and patchy coverage outside the cities. Whatever you put online has to work on an ordinary phone with a weak signal.

## आफ्नो माध्यम र भाडाको माध्यम / Owned channels and rented ones

Facebook page, Instagram, TikTok र YouTube channel भाडाको पसलजस्तै हुन्। ठाउँ राम्रो छ, मान्छे धेरै आउँछन्, तर घरबेटी अर्कै हो। नियम फेरियो, account रोकियो वा platform नै बन्द भयो भने तपाईंको हातमा केही रहँदैन।

यो कल्पना मात्र होइन। सन् २०२५ सेप्टेम्बर ४ मा सरकारले दर्ता नभएका २६ वटा platform मा रोक लगायो, जसमा Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp र YouTube पनि थिए। TikTok र Viber दर्ता भइसकेकाले चलिरहे। रोक सेप्टेम्बर ९ मा हट्यो। त्यसअघि TikTok आफैं सन् २०२३ नोभेम्बरदेखि २०२४ अगस्टसम्म बन्द थियो।

आफ्नो माध्यम भनेको आफ्नै domain मा चलेको website र ग्राहकको अनुमति लिएर राखेको फोन नम्बर वा email को सूची हो। यसको नियन्त्रण तपाईंकै हातमा हुन्छ। भाडाको माध्यम छोड्नु पर्दैन, नयाँ ग्राहक त्यहीँ भेटिन्छन्। तर तिनलाई बिस्तारै आफ्नो माध्यमतिर ल्याउनुहोस्।

A Facebook page, an Instagram account, a TikTok or YouTube channel: these are rented shops. Good location, plenty of foot traffic, but somebody else owns the building. If the rules change or the platform goes dark, there isn't much you can do about it.

That isn't hypothetical in Nepal. On 4 September 2025 the government [blocked 26 unregistered platforms](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2025/09/04/nepal-bans-facebook-and-other-major-social-media-platforms-over-non-compliance), including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and YouTube. TikTok and Viber were registered and stayed up. The block was lifted on 9 September. Before that, TikTok itself was [banned from November 2023 to August 2024](https://kathmandupost.com/national/2024/08/23/nepal-lifts-its-ban-on-tiktok). A business that lived entirely on one of those platforms lost its main line to customers overnight.

| | Owned | Rented |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Your website and domain, a customer phone or email list | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, marketplace listings |
| Who sets the rules | You | The platform, and sometimes the government |
| Best used for | Being found, trusted and contacted, whatever happens elsewhere | Reaching new people |

Rented channels are still worth using, because most new customers will find you there first. The point is to use them to move people towards the things you own, so one policy change doesn't close your shop. I've written separately about [whether a small business needs a website or whether a Facebook page is enough](/blog/website-vs-facebook-page-nepal).

## पहिलो ९० दिनको सजिलो योजना / A simple plan for the first 90 days

### दिन १–३०: आधार बनाउने / Days 1–30: get the basics right

तपाईंको मुख्य ग्राहक को हो र उनीहरूलाई के बेच्ने, एक वाक्यमा लेख्नुहोस्। एउटा website बनाउनुहोस्, एक page मात्र भए पनि, जहाँ फोन नम्बर, ठेगाना, खुल्ने समय, मूल्य वा मूल्यको दायरा, र Viber वा WhatsApp मा सिधै message गर्ने button होस्। Google, Facebook र Instagram मा यही जानकारी मिल्दो बनाउनुहोस्। अनि एउटा कापी वा spreadsheet खोल्नुहोस्: हरेक सोधपुछको मिति, कहाँबाट थाहा पाएको, के सोधेको र किने कि किनेनन्।

Write down, in one sentence, who your main customer is and what you're selling them. Get a website up, even a single page, with your phone number, address, opening hours, prices or price ranges, and a button that opens Viber or WhatsApp directly. Make sure the same details match on Google, Facebook and Instagram. A different phone number on each is more common than you'd think. Limi Creatives has a guide to [getting the Google side of this right](https://limicreatives.com/blogs/local-seo-for-restaurants-and-retail).

Then open a notebook or a spreadsheet. For every enquiry, write the date, where they heard about you, what they asked, and whether they bought. This one habit will teach you more than any dashboard.

### दिन ३१–६०: ग्राहकसँग कुरा सुरु गर्ने / Days 31–60: start the conversation

तपाईंका ग्राहक सबैभन्दा बढी जहाँ छन्, त्यही एक वा दुई platform रोज्नुहोस्। सबै ठाउँमा आधा मन लगाएर हुनुभन्दा दुई ठाउँमा राम्रोसँग हुनु ठिक। साँचो कुरा देखाउनुहोस्: सामान, मूल्य, र ग्राहकले बारम्बार सोध्ने प्रश्नको जवाफ। हरेक message को जवाफ छिटो दिनुहोस्। किनेका ग्राहकलाई सोध्नुहोस्, र उनीहरू राजी भए मात्र Viber वा SMS सूचीमा राख्नुहोस्।

Pick the one or two platforms where your customers already spend time, and be properly present there rather than half-present everywhere. Show real things: the product, the price, the answer to the question people keep asking in your inbox. Reply to every message quickly, because a slow reply can lose a sale the post had already won. When someone buys, ask whether they'd like to hear about new stock or offers on Viber or SMS, and add them only if they say yes.

How often to post is its own question, and [Limi's post on why posting daily isn't a strategy](https://limicreatives.com/blogs/posting-daily-is-not-a-strategy) covers it.

### दिन ६१–९०: हेर्ने र सुधार्ने / Days 61–90: look back and adjust

अब कापी खोल्नुहोस्। कुन माध्यमबाट आएका सोधपुछ बिक्रीमा बदलिए? त्यसमा बढी समय दिनुहोस्। जसबाट केही आएन, त्यो घटाउनुहोस् वा छोड्नुहोस्। विज्ञापन चलाउने सोच छ भने यो सही समय हो, किनकि अब कुन ग्राहक र कुन सामानमा पैसा हाल्ने भन्ने तपाईंलाई थाहा छ।

Open the notebook. Which channel brought enquiries that turned into sales? Put more time there, and cut back on whatever brought nothing. If you want to try paid ads, this is a sensible point to start, because you now know which customer and which product are worth putting money behind. For how much to spend, see [Limi's guide to ad budgets in Nepal](https://limicreatives.com/blogs/what-a-healthy-ad-budget-looks-like-in-nepal).

None of this needs an agency. It needs a phone, a notebook and ninety days of paying attention.

## काम भयो कि भएन, कसरी नाप्ने? / How to measure it (not in likes)

Like, follower र view हेर्दा राम्रो लाग्छ, तर तिनले पसलको भाडा तिर्दैनन्। नाप्नुपर्ने कुरा यी हुन्: कति जनाले सोधपुछ गरे (message, फोन वा form), तीमध्ये कतिले किने, र कति जना फेरि आए। हरेक नयाँ ग्राहकलाई "हामीलाई कहाँबाट थाहा पाउनुभयो?" भनेर सोध्नुहोस् र लेखेर राख्नुहोस्। यो सबैभन्दा सस्तो र भरपर्दो analytics हो।

Likes, followers and views feel good. They don't pay the rent. The numbers worth watching are closer to money:

- **Enquiries:** messages, calls, form submissions, and walk-ins who saw you online
- **Sales:** how many of those enquiries actually bought
- **Repeat customers:** how many came back, which is where owned channels earn their keep

Ask every new customer "हामीलाई कहाँबाट थाहा पाउनुभयो?" (how did you hear about us?) and write the answer down. It's the cheapest and most reliable analytics you'll ever have. Free tools like Google Analytics for your website and Meta Business Suite for your pages can back the notebook up, but they don't replace it.

A post with thousands of likes and no enquiries did less for the business than a plain one that brought a couple of phone calls.

## प्रायः सोधिने प्रश्न / Frequently asked questions

### Facebook boost गर्नु नै डिजिटल मार्केटिङ हो? / Is boosting on Facebook the same as digital marketing?

होइन। Boost एउटा औजार मात्र हो। तपाईंको website, Google मा कसरी देखिनुहुन्छ, ग्राहकको सूची र message को कति छिटो जवाफ दिनुहुन्छ, यी सबै डिजिटल मार्केटिङ नै हुन्, र यीमध्ये धेरैमा पैसा लाग्दैन।

No. Boosting is one tool. Your website, how you show up on Google, your customer list and how fast you answer messages are all part of it, and several of them cost nothing.

### "Digital marketing vaneko k ho" र "bhaneko k ho" फरक प्रश्न हुन्? / Is "vaneko k ho" a different question?

होइन, एउटै प्रश्न हो। "भनेको" लाई Roman अक्षरमा कसैले "bhaneko" लेख्छन्, कसैले "vaneko"। दुवैको अर्थ एउटै हो: डिजिटल मार्केटिङको मतलब के हो?

Same question. People romanise भनेको as "bhaneko" or "vaneko" depending on habit; both are asking for the meaning of digital marketing in Nepali.

### सानो पसललाई पनि website चाहिन्छ? / Does a small shop really need a website?

सबैलाई ठूलो website चाहिँदैन। तर आफ्नै domain मा सम्पर्क, ठेगाना र मूल्य भएको एउटा सफा page चाहिँ हुनुपर्छ। Platform बन्द हुँदा पनि ग्राहकले तपाईंलाई भेट्ने ठाउँ त्यही हो।

Not everyone needs a big one. But a simple page on your own domain with your contact details, location and prices is where customers can still find you when a platform goes down. More in [website vs Facebook page](/blog/website-vs-facebook-page-nepal).

### नतिजा देखिन कति समय लाग्छ? / How long before it starts working?

माध्यमअनुसार फरक पर्छ। पहिलो ९० दिन नतिजा जाँच्ने भन्दा पनि के काम गर्छ भनेर सिक्ने समय हो। Google search को समयबारे Limi Creatives को छुट्टै लेख छ।

It depends on the channel. Treat the first 90 days as time to learn what works, not the moment to judge the whole thing. For search, Limi's post on [how long SEO actually takes in Nepal](https://limicreatives.com/blogs/how-long-does-seo-actually-take-in-nepal) covers the timeline.
